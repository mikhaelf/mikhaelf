# Mikhael Felker

Security, privacy and AI governance leader with 20+ years across health tech, finance and consumer technology. Most recently Head of Product Security & Privacy Engineering (ISSO) at Verily (Alphabet). Design partner to ArmorIQ on agentic AI runtime security.

This is where I build in public: practitioner tools and references for securing agentic AI, especially where it touches regulated data. I build hands-on, mostly with Claude Code, and every agent I ship runs behind human-in-the-loop controls.
